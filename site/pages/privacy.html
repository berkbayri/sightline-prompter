Privacy Policy

Sightline Prompter for iPhone and Mac. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Summary

Sightline Prompter does not collect, store or sell any personal data. It has no account, no advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites. It counts anonymous usage (see Anonymous usage statistics below). Everything you create stays on your device or in your own iCloud account.

What the app accesses

  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used to follow the words you read and move the script. Where the language's on-device model is installed, recognition runs on your device. Otherwise Apple's Speech framework may send the audio to Apple to be recognized, under Apple's privacy policy. The developer never receives this audio.
  • Camera (iPhone). Shows the live preview and records your video. Video is not sent anywhere.
  • Photos (iPhone, add only). Finished recordings are saved to your Photos library. The app cannot read your other photos.
  • Files you choose. Scripts you import are read only when you select them.

Where your data lives

Scripts are stored as files on your device, and on the Mac optionally in iCloud Drive. Recordings are stored in your Photos library. The developer has no access to any of it.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends short event counts, such as app opened, recording saved, free limit reached, paywall shown and purchase completed, with the app version, the platform and a random ID created on install. The ID is not linked to your Apple Account, your device or any advertising ID. Scripts, voice, audio, video and photos are never sent. The events go to PostHog and Google Analytics. Turn this off any time in Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous usage.

Purchases

If the app offers a paid upgrade, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details or personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
