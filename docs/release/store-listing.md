## Açıklama: iPhone

```
Read your script while looking at the lens.

Sightline Prompter puts a short band of text right under the front camera, so your eyes stay near the lens while you record. A small preview of your video sits below it, with the record button and camera controls.

VOICE FOLLOWING
The script moves with your voice. Speak faster and it keeps up, pause and it waits. It only matches words that are on screen, so it never jumps to the wrong place. Works through notifications and picks up where you are if you scroll to another part.

MADE FOR THE CAMERA
• A narrow, short prompter band you can size to the pixel, so your eye movement stays small on the recording
• Portrait and landscape, with the text always next to the lens
• 16:9 or 4:3, up to 4K, 60 fps, HDR, HEVC, H.264 and ProRes
• Exposure, focus and white balance locks, torch, zoom, stabilization
• Recordings are saved to your Photos library

31 LANGUAGES
English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Cantonese, Turkish and more. Many languages recognize your speech on device.

YOUR SCRIPTS
Write, import (text, Markdown, Word, PDF, RTF, HTML, OpenDocument), search and edit scripts. Control the reading speed and show words per minute and time left.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your scripts and videos stay on your device.
```

## Açıklama: Mac

```
A teleprompter that lives under the MacBook notch.

Keep your eyes at the camera while you read. Sightline Prompter places your script right under the notch and follows your voice, so the text moves at your pace.

• Voice following in 31 languages and regional variants, with offline recognition where available
• Hover over the panel for controls; click the text to play and pause
• PlayStation DualSense controller support: analog stick scrolling, speed, paragraph jumps

• Script library with import from text, Markdown, Word, PDF, RTF, HTML and OpenDocument

No account, no analytics, no tracking.
```

## İnceleme notları (App Review Information → Notes)

**iPhone:**
```
Sightline Prompter is a teleprompter that records video. Open the app, tap any script in the list (a sample script is included), then allow camera, microphone and speech recognition when asked. The prompter band appears under the front camera and the preview and record button are below it. Speech recognition follows the spoken words to scroll the script. Recordings are saved to Photos (add-only access). The app needs no account or login.
```

**Mac:**
```
Sightline Prompter for Mac is a menu bar style app: it has no Dock icon and no main window. After launch the teleprompter panel appears at the top center of the screen, under the notch (on Macs without a notch it sits at the top of the screen). Hover the pointer over the panel to show its controls. Click the text to play or pause. Turn on speech tracking with the microphone button and allow microphone and speech recognition access when asked. To open the script library or settings, use the buttons on the control bar. The app needs no account or login.
```
