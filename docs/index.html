Sightline Prompter is a teleprompter app for iPhone and Mac. It follows your voice by matching only the words on screen, so a cough, an ad-lib or a skipped line does not throw it. The text stays right under the camera. Free to try.

See how it works

One script. Two devices.

Write on your Mac, record on your iPhone. Both apps share one iCloud script library, so the script is already on your phone when you press record. It is one app on the App Store, and one purchase covers both.

  • iPhone, over the live preview

Sized to your distance.

On iPhone, the app estimates how far your face is from the camera, then sets the text size, line length and number of lines to match. Move back and the text grows, lean in and it shrinks. You read comfortably while your eyes stay near the lens.

Teleprompter questions

How does a teleprompter that follows your voice work?

Sightline Prompter listens while you read and matches what it hears to the words on screen. The text moves when you speak and waits when you pause, so you set the pace instead of a scroll speed. It only matches words that are in your script, so a cough, an ad-lib or a skipped line does not make it jump.

Is there a free teleprompter app that follows your voice?

Yes. The free version follows your voice for up to 2 minutes per run in English, Turkish and Spanish, and records up to 2 minutes of 1080p video on iPhone. Sightline Pro removes the limits.

Does voice following work offline?

Many languages are recognized on the device, so they work without a connection. If a language has no offline model installed, the audio goes to Apple's speech service, and the app says so on screen. Turkish needs the iOS Dictation model for on-device recognition.

Which languages does it support?

31 languages and regional variants, including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Cantonese and Turkish. The free version covers English, Turkish and Spanish. Chinese, Japanese and Cantonese are matched in segments, so accuracy is lower than in languages written with spaces.

What if I go off script?

It stays where you are. A cough, an ad-lib or a skipped line does not make the text jump. It only moves when you say words that are on screen.

Do I need a MacBook with a notch?

No. On a MacBook with a notch, the panel sits right below it. On a Mac without one, the panel sits at the top center of the screen.

How does auto text size work?

On iPhone, the front camera estimates how far your face is from the screen and sizes the text so the letters keep the same visual angle, which keeps them comfortable to read at that distance. It also limits line length and the number of lines, so your eyes stay near the lens. On Mac, you choose a distance preset or turn on the optional camera measurement. Everything is measured on your device, and no video or face data leaves it.

Does it record video?

Yes, on iPhone. The app records with the script floating over the live preview and saves the video to your Photos library. The Mac app is a prompter panel for calls and recordings you make with other tools.

Does one purchase cover iPhone and Mac?

Yes. It is one app on the App Store, and Sightline Pro unlocks both the iPhone and the Mac version. Both use the same iCloud script library.

What does Sightline Pro cost?

Sightline Pro is available as a subscription (with a free trial) or as a one-time purchase. The App Store shows current prices in your local currency.

Which files can I import?

Plain text, Markdown, Word, PDF, RTF, HTML and OpenDocument files. Scripts sync between iPhone and Mac through iCloud.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes. A DualSense controller works with the Mac app over Bluetooth or USB: the analog stick scrolls, and buttons change speed and jump between paragraphs. Controller support is part of Sightline Pro.

What happens to my data?

Your scripts stay in your own iCloud Drive. There are no ads, no Sightline account and no tracking across apps. Sync uses your iCloud account, so you need to be signed in to iCloud on the device. Anonymous usage counts can be switched off in Settings.
