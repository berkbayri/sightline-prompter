## İnceleme notları (App Review Information → Notes)

**iPhone:**
```
Sightline Prompter is a teleprompter that records video. Open the app, tap any script in the list (a sample script is included), then allow camera, microphone and speech recognition when asked. The prompter band appears under the front camera and the preview and record button are below it. Speech recognition follows the spoken words to scroll the script. Recordings are saved to Photos (add-only access). The app has no account of its own, but it needs the device to be signed in to iCloud (Settings > Apple Account > iCloud Drive on), because scripts are stored in the app's iCloud container and shared with the Mac app. Without an iCloud account the app shows a sign-in screen. Free use is limited to 2 minutes of voice following and recording per run; Sightline Pro (in-app purchase) removes the limits.
```

**Mac:**
```
Sightline Prompter for Mac is a menu bar style app: it has no Dock icon and no main window. After launch the teleprompter panel appears at the top center of the screen, under the notch (on Macs without a notch it sits at the top of the screen). Hover the pointer over the panel to show its controls. Click the text to play or pause. Turn on speech tracking with the microphone button and allow microphone and speech recognition access when asked. To open the script library or settings, use the buttons on the control bar. The app has no account of its own, but it needs the Mac to be signed in to iCloud (System Settings > Apple Account > iCloud Drive on), because scripts are stored in the app's iCloud container. Without an iCloud account the app shows a sign-in window. Free use is limited to 2 minutes of voice following per run; Sightline Pro (in-app purchase) removes the limits.
```
