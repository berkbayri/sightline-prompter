Support

Help with Sightline Prompter for iPhone and Mac.

Contact

Write to hi@sightlineprompter.app. Tell us your device, your iOS or macOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

Voice following, languages, offline use, file import and controllers are answered in the FAQ on the home page.

Restore a purchase

Bought Sightline Pro on another device, or reinstalled the app? Open Settings > Sightline Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. One purchase covers iPhone and Mac.

Privacy and terms

How the app and this site handle data is in the Privacy Policy. The license and subscription terms are in the Terms of Use.
