# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Content creators who read a script to a camera: video, social and course recordings. They want to keep their eyes on the lens while the text stays just below it. Confirmed as the primary audience; presenters and speakers are not the focus.

## Product Purpose

Sightline Prompter shows a script as a teleprompter on iPhone and Mac and follows the speaker's voice word by word, so the text advances at the reader's pace. Success is a recording where the speaker looks at the camera and never loses their place.

## Positioning

The prompter that follows the voice, word by word, instead of scrolling at a fixed speed. Confirmed as the difference a neighboring product could not copy.

## Operating Context

- iPhone: a camera mode that records with the prompter text floating over the live preview, portrait and landscape, with the reading line placed under the camera lens.
- Mac: a floating panel near the camera notch for reading while on a call or recording.
- Scripts live in one shared iCloud library, so the same files appear on both devices. An iCloud sign-in is required to use the app.
- Optional DualSense controller for scrolling.
- Delivery cues written as `[[...]]` blocks are shown but never read aloud or counted as words.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Free tier: voice following and recording up to 2 minutes, 1080p at 30 fps, languages English, Turkish and Spanish. Limits come from `FreeTier`.
- Sightline Pro: no limits, 4K at 60 fps, HDR, ProRes, 31 languages, adjustable prompter size, pace readout, controller tuning. One purchase covers iPhone and Mac. Prices: $4.99 monthly, $29.99 yearly with a 7-day trial, $59.99 lifetime.
- Developer and debug builds unlock Pro and skip the sign-in gate.
- In camera mode the preview and text must never overlap (`ArrangementGeometry`).
- Undecided: App Store listing screenshots and final marketing copy.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Sightline Prompter. A yellow accent marks the word being read and the Pro offer. No other brand rules were confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

Store listing and pricing notes in `docs/release/`. There are no customer testimonials, press or benchmarks; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The reader's eyes stay on the lens; nothing competes with the text under it.
2. The free version proves the value; Pro removes limits without hiding the core idea.
3. One library, two devices, no setup beyond signing in to iCloud.
4. Upgrade prompts explain what is gained and never interrupt a take already running.
