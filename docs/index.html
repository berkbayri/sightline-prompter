Sightline Prompter

A free, open-source teleprompter under the MacBook notch. It follows your voice, so the script moves with you.

Download for macOS

Why it is different

  • Voice tracking that only matches the words on screen, so it never jumps to another part of the script.
  • Under the notch, right next to the camera, so you read while looking at the lens.
  • Speech in 31 languages and regional variants: every language Apple offers for one-click offline download, plus Turkish.
  • Offline speech for every language whose model is on the Mac, with downloads from Settings where Apple allows them.
  • PS5 DualSense control: analog stick scrolling with an adjustable curve, D-pad speed, and a light bar that shows the state.
  • Words per minute and time left, measured from your reading.
  • Script library in iCloud Drive, with import from text, Markdown, RTF, HTML, Word, OpenDocument and PDF.

Install

Download the zip from the latest release, move the app to Applications, and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Sightline Prompter.app" once. The app is not notarized, so this step is needed.
